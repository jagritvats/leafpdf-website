Guides

How to get common PDF jobs done with Leaf, step by step, on your own computer. Each guide uses the menu names and shortcuts in Leaf 0.2.2 and says plainly what Leaf can't do.

  • Edit the text in a PDF, offline and free Change the words already on the page, in the document's own font, with no upload.
  • Redact a PDF permanently, so the text is really gone Remove text, images and hidden data for real, and find every email or phone number first.
  • Fill in and sign a PDF form without uploading it Type into fields, tick boxes and add a drawn, typed or scanned signature.
  • Merge PDFs, then reorder, rotate and extract pages Combine files into one, then reorder, rotate, extract or split pages.
  • Make a scanned PDF searchable, with OCR that runs offline Turn scanned pages into text you can search, select and copy, without uploading them.
  • Add a digital signature to a PDF with your certificate Sign with a .p12 or .pfx certificate, or one Leaf creates, and check other signatures offline.
  • Edit PDFs on Linux with Leaf Install the AppImage or the tarball, then edit, redact, sign and OCR PDFs offline.
  • Why use an offline PDF editor What happens to a file when you edit it online, and when keeping it local matters.

Leaf is free and open source, for Windows 10 and 11 and Linux. Something missing or wrong? Open an issue on GitHub.
